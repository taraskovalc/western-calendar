1. Who runs Slotted

Slotted (slotted.taraskoval.ca) is a free, independent tool made and run by Taras Koval, a student in London, Ontario, Canada. It turns a Western University DraftMySchedule page into calendar events. Slotted isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Western University. In this policy, "Slotted", "I" and "me" refer to Taras Koval, and "you" means anyone who uses Slotted.

2. What Slotted collects

If you use Slotted without an account

Nothing is collected. Your saved schedule page is read by the website in your browser, and your calendar file is created on your device. Neither leaves your device.

If you create an account

If you report a problem

Your message is emailed to Slotted's inbox, along with your browser and device type (for example "Safari on iPhone"), whether you were signed in, and anything you choose to add: your email address and the class list from your schedule. The schedule file itself is never sent; Slotted reads it in your browser and sends only the classes it found, as plain text. Reports aren't stored on Slotted's server.

Technical information

To protect against abuse, Slotted's server briefly uses your IP address to limit how often the same address can try things like signing in. These counts are kept in memory only and reset within an hour. Slotted's hosting and security providers also handle IP addresses as part of delivering the site (see Service providers).

The website also saves a few small settings in your own browser: your light or dark mode choice, and short-lived notes that only last while a tab is open. These never leave your browser.

3. The browser extension

The optional Slotted for DraftMySchedule extension adds a Send to Slotted button to DraftMySchedule. It only runs on DraftMySchedule's My Current Schedule page and on Slotted itself.

  • When you click the button, it copies that one page (your class list, which includes your name) and hands it to the Slotted tab it opens.
  • The copy stays in your browser. It's held in the extension's temporary storage and deleted as soon as Slotted takes it, or after 5 minutes if it's never used.
  • It's never sent to a server. The extension doesn't read any other page, track your browsing, or collect anything else.

4. Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Slotted asks Google only for your name, email address and an account ID number, and uses them only to create your Slotted account and recognise you when you sign in again. Slotted never sees your Google password and doesn't ask for access to your calendar, contacts, files or anything else.

Slotted's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove Slotted's access at any time from your Google Account under Security → Your connections to third-party apps.

5. How information is used

Information is used only to provide Slotted: to sign you in, save your schedules, serve your calendar link, send account emails, answer problem reports, and keep the service secure. Slotted does not:

  • show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools;
  • sell, rent or trade your information;
  • share it with anyone except the service providers below, who only process it to run Slotted;
  • use it to train AI models.

Information may be disclosed only if required by law.

7. Storage and security

Account information is stored on a server operated by Taras Koval in London, Ontario, Canada. The server isn't directly exposed to the internet; it's reached only through an encrypted Cloudflare connection, and the site is served over HTTPS only. Passwords are hashed with Argon2, calendar links use long random codes, sign-in and reset attempts are rate-limited, and the database is backed up daily. No system is perfectly secure, but Slotted is built to collect as little as possible so there's little to protect.

9. Your choices and rights

  • See or change your information: sign in to view your saved schedules, rename or update them, or change your settings.
  • Delete everything: sign in, open Account at the top of the page, and choose Delete my account. Your account, schedules and calendar links are removed immediately, and from backups within 14 days.
  • Turn off a calendar link: you can turn it off or replace it at any time; the old link stops working right away.
  • Ask a question or request a copy of your information: email slotted@taraskoval.ca. You'll get a reply within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with the response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Children

Slotted is made for university students and isn't intended for children under 13. Slotted doesn't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email slotted@taraskoval.ca and it will be deleted.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Significant changes, such as collecting a new kind of information, will also be announced on Slotted before they take effect.
