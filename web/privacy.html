Privacy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Slotted turns a saved Western University DraftMySchedule page into calendar events. This page explains what it stores, why, and how to delete it.

Without an account

Nothing is stored. Your schedule file is read by the page in your browser, and the calendar file is made on your device. Neither is sent to a server.

With an account

  • Your email, your name if you gave one, and your password, stored only as a scrambled hash that can't be turned back into the password.
  • Your saved schedules: each class's course code and title, section, class number, instructor, delivery type, days, times and rooms, plus your settings (term, reminder, calendar app, light or dark mode).
  • Your private calendar link, if you made one, and when your calendar app last checked it.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days. It's only used for signing in.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Slotted asks Google only for your name, your email address and an account ID number. They're used for one thing: creating your Slotted account and recognising you when you sign in again.

Slotted never sees your Google password, and doesn't ask for access to your calendar, contacts, files or anything else. The information isn't shared with anyone, isn't used for ads, and isn't used to train AI models. Slotted's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Slotted doesn't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and your information is never sold or shared. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

Where it's kept

On a server run by Taras Koval in London, Ontario, reached through Cloudflare. It's backed up once a day, and backups are kept for 14 days.

Deleting it

Sign in, open Account at the top of the page, and choose Delete my account. Your account, schedules and calendar links are removed straight away, and from backups within 14 days. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove Slotted's access at any time from your Google Account under Security → Your connections to third-party apps.
