    Save your schedule from DraftMySchedule

    Slotted needs one file: your DraftMySchedule page, saved from your browser. Four steps, about a minute. Tick each one off as you go.

    1. Go to draftmyschedule.uwo.ca (opens in a new tab) and sign in with your Western account. Use a laptop or desktop: phones can't save a whole web page.

    2. After you sign in, DraftMySchedule opens on Build Draft. That page is for planning drafts, not the classes you're registered in, so switch to My Current Schedule.

      HomeCourse ListBuild DraftAccess Saved DraftsMy Shared DraftsMy Current Schedule← click this oneEnrollment Appointment

    3. Stay on My Current Schedule; there's no need to scroll or click anything else. Saving keeps the whole page, including the class list Slotted reads.

      Press Ctrl + S, set the format to Webpage, HTML Only, then click Save.

      Steps for other browsers
      • Chrome or Edge: Ctrl + S (⌘ + S on a Mac), set the format to Webpage, HTML Only, then Save.
      • Safari: choose File → Save As…, set Format to Page Source, then Save.
      • Firefox: Ctrl + S (⌘ + S on a Mac), choose Web Page, HTML only, then Save.

      Save the page itself, not DraftMySchedule's PDF. The PDF doesn't keep the rooms and times in a form Slotted can read.

    4. It's usually in your Downloads folder, named The University of Western Ontario.html.

        1. Open the file. On a Mac, double-click it in Downloads. On an iPhone or iPad, tap it in the Files app or in your downloads.
        2. Choose where the classes go. On a Mac, pick New Calendar so your classes have their own calendar you can hide, recolour or remove in one go. On iPhone or iPad, tap Add All.
        3. That's it. With iCloud turned on, your classes appear on your other Apple devices automatically.
        1. On a computer, go to calendar.google.com. Google only imports files on a computer; afterwards your classes show up on your phone too.
        2. Make a calendar just for classes. Go to Settings → Add calendar → Create new calendar and name it something like “Western Classes”. Google can't undo an import, so a separate calendar lets you delete all the classes in one step if you ever need to.
        3. Import the file. Go to Settings → Import & export, select the downloaded file, choose your new calendar, then click Import.
        4. Turn on reminders (optional). Open your new calendar's settings and add an Event notification, for example 10 minutes before.

        3. The reminder you picked is included and works in Outlook.

        What Slotted stores

        Without an account

        Nothing. Your schedule file is read by this page in your browser, and the calendar file is made on your device. Neither is sent to a server.

        With an account

        • Your email, your name if you gave one, and your password stored only as a scrambled hash that can't be turned back into the password.
        • Your saved schedules: each class's course code and title, section, class number, instructor, delivery type, days, times and rooms, plus your settings (term, reminder, calendar app, light or dark mode).
        • Your private calendar link, if you made one, and when your calendar app last checked it.
        • If you use Google to sign in: an ID number from Google so it can recognise you. Slotted never sees your Google password.
        • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days. It's only used for signing in.

        What Slotted doesn't do

        No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and your information is never sold or shared. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

        Where it's kept

        On a server run by Taras Koval in London, Ontario, reached through Cloudflare. It's backed up once a day, and backups are kept for 14 days.

        Problem reports

        A report you send with “Report a problem” is emailed to Taras with whatever you add to it. It isn't stored on the server.

        Deleting it

        Sign in, open Account at the top of the page, and choose Delete my account. Your account, schedules and calendar links are removed straight away (and from backups within 14 days). Questions: slotted@taraskoval.ca.
